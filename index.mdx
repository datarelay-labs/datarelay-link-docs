## Mental model

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    O["Operator / approved source"] --> S["DataRelay Link Server"]
    A["Managed Host / Agent"] -->|"outbound relay"| S
    S -->|"Remote Service"| A
    A --> T["Local or LAN target"]
    H["Restricted application host"] -->|"HTTP(S) proxy - agentless"| S
    S --> I["Approved Internet destination"]
    AI["Authenticated AI Identity"] -->|"AI Access / MCP"| S
```

The key distinction is simple:

- **Remote Service** creates or represents connectivity.
- **Access Policy** decides whether that connectivity may be used.
- **Rules do not create Remote Services.**

For the complete object model, policy behavior, and role boundaries, read [Concepts & Mental Model](/getting-started/concepts).

## Policy defaults — three separate access controls

- **Remote Access:** No Policy/No Rules initially means **ALLOW**. In BLACKLIST, matching rules DENY and unmatched traffic ALLOW; in WHITELIST, matching rules ALLOW and unmatched traffic DENY. Disabled enforcement means **ALLOW ALL**.
- **Internet Access:** **WHITELIST only**. No matching enabled rule, no policy, or disabled enforcement means **DENY ALL**. BLACKLIST is not available.
- **AI Access:** **WHITELIST only**. Without an authenticated AI Identity and matching grant, access is **DENY**; disabled enforcement also means **DENY ALL**.
- Rules have no priority ordering or individual allow/deny action. Resetting a policy removes Mode/Rules and restores that plane's initial behavior. **Never disable Remote Access enforcement to lock down a published service.**

## Product boundary

DataRelay Link relays explicitly configured services and approved destinations. It is not a full VPN, transparent network overlay, automatic cloud firewall/DNS manager, or large fleet-management platform.

External firewall/NAT rules, cloud security groups, DNS-provider records, OS accounts, and application certificates remain operator responsibilities unless a separately documented feature says otherwise.

For supported product boundaries and protocol limitations, see [Known Limits](/reference/limitations).
